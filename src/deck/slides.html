The fix · 1 of 6

One loop, four moves.

Diagnose

Cumulative sentiment with a walk-away point, so a call can be lost.

Prescribe

A report naming the one thing that cost the call, and the drill for it.

Drill

Coverage across eight customers, plus scenarios the rep writes himself.

Prove

A profile and a GM view that put practice next to the floor. The CRM and the call recordings are what turn it into proof, not the practice log.

Detection is a compliance function. Coaching has to prescribe, drill, and prove.
